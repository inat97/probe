Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

The short version. Probe does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your checks, temperature readings and diary entries are stored on your phone and never leave it, except in the files you deliberately export.

1. Who this policy is from

Probe is developed by Nguyen Anh Tuan ("we"). Contact: 1551010058.linh+probe@gmail.com

2. What Probe stores, and where

Probe stores the records you enter: your business name and address line, the name you sign checks with, the units you check and their target ranges, your checklists, temperature readings with the time they were taken and the time they were typed, the actions you record when a reading is outside target, your sign-offs, and your diary entries. All of it is written to your device's local storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. We have no database, no server, and no way to read it.

3. Records about other people

Most of what Probe holds is about equipment, not people. But a diary entry is a free text box, and entries such as a member of staff being sent home unwell, or the name of a delivery driver, are personal data about someone else. Those entries stay on your device exactly like everything else — we never receive them. In data protection terms you are the controller of anything you write in there: record only what you actually need, and be careful about health information, which is treated as a special category almost everywhere.

4. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address, or profile.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, or event logging.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, calendar, or motion data.

5. Files you export

When you export a PDF pack or a CSV, Probe writes it to a temporary folder on your device and hands it to whatever you choose next — Mail, Files, a printer, a messaging app. Where it goes after that is governed by the app you sent it with, not by Probe.

6. Notifications

The optional opening and closing reminders are scheduled entirely on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent through a push server, and no reminder ever leaves the phone.

7. Payments

The Probe subscription is sold by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details, and receive only aggregated, non-identifying sales reports.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your records stay on your device until you delete them. Deleting the app removes them.

Because there is no server, we cannot restore anything. If the phone is lost, broken or reset, the only copies are your device backup and the packs you have already exported. Food safety records are commonly expected to be kept for a long period after the day they describe — do not let a single phone be the only place they exist. Export at the end of every month and keep the file somewhere else.

9. Children

Probe is a record-keeping tool for food businesses and is not directed to children.

10. Your rights

Laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data held about you. We hold none. Your records live on your device under your control. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

11. Changes to this policy

The current version is always at https://inat97.github.io/probe/privacy.html.
