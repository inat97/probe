Probe

The daily food safety book for a kitchen where one person does the checks.

Opening and closing checks, fridge and freezer temperatures, and the diary line for the day something went wrong. Probe keeps them in one place, shows you the days that are missing, and turns any period into a single PDF you can hand over.

One thing it does that a paper book cannot: a temperature outside its target cannot be saved until you write what you did about it. That blank is the one that gets asked about.

No account, no server, no analytics. Everything stays on the phone.
